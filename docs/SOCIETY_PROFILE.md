# DVD Tivat operating profile

Status: **confirmed first-hand on 9 September 2026 by the prototype owner, a DVD Tivat
firefighter-rescuer.** These are operating facts, not formal approval of the future application or
its proposed permissions. This document contains public-safe facts only. The public repository must
never contain real member names, telephone numbers, home addresses, registration plates,
credentials or incident records.

## Facts used by the prototype

| Area | Current profile |
|---|---|
| Intended organisation | DVD Tivat only |
| Reported membership | 52 members |
| Shift model | No shifts; members are normally at home |
| Assembly flow | When called, members come to the DVD Tivat base, collect equipment, then deploy |
| Reported vehicle categories | One MAN firefighting vehicle and one firefighting SUV |
| Current alert channel | A Viber group |
| Member phones | Both iPhone and Android |

The 52 rows in the prototype are fictional. The first fourteen have readable demonstration names;
the remaining rows are deliberately generic (`Probni clan 15` through `Probni clan 52`). Vehicle
callsigns are also fictional and no registration plate is stored.

The Viber group is shown only as the existing fallback channel. The prototype does not connect to,
read from or send through Viber. It sends no notification of any kind.

## Product decisions still needed before production

1. Who receives the first report and who may create, cancel, change or close a call-out?
2. Is assembly at the base mandatory for every member, with no direct-to-incident exception?
3. Are the three response choices and 15/30/60-minute bands correct?
4. Should Viber stay as the tested fallback during pilot exercises, and what happens without data?
5. Is citizen reporting wanted, who monitors it and which official emergency instruction is shown?
6. May an approved crest be used, and can the society supply the original SVG or high-resolution PNG?

The operating profile above does not need a second factual confirmation. The decisions in this
section still need an authorised product decision before they become production rules. Until that
happens, this remains a final-quality **workflow prototype**, not an operational emergency system.
